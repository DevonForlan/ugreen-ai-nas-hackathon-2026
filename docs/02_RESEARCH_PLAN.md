## R05 — User Problems & Market Opportunities

研究：找出適合 NAS + AI Agent 的高價值問題。

評估：

- User pain
- Existing solution
- Why NAS
- Why AI
- Why Agent
- Privacy advantage
- Local execution advantage
- Automation value
- Measurable outcome

## R06 — Candidate Idea Evaluation

**R01–R05 研究完成後才開始。**

使用官方四項評分標準：

- User Value
- Innovation & Differentiation
- Technical Quality
- NAS Integration & Validation

另外加入：

- Development difficulty
- Dependency risk
- Demo quality
- Time risk
- Existing UGREEN feature overlap
