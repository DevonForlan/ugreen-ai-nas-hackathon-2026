## A. Executive verdict

**NAS-NATIVE FIT NOT ESTABLISHED**

**FACT:** Professional NAS/shared-storage usage is real, including engineering projects, scientific imaging, production media and museum collections. **INFERENCE:** This establishes an operational data home, but does not establish a differentiated Evidence-first product. No researched cohort has yet been observed jointly satisfying authoritative local originals, recurrent review, an incumbent-resistant gap and an advantage over a file-aware general AI using the same authorized files.

**Strongest supporting evidence:** Field Museum's institutional technical documentation connects NAS/archive storage, core collection media and EMu records within one architecture [S24]. This is stronger than an unrelated storage testimonial plus another profession's pain article. It still does not prove an unmet loan-review job there. UC Davis independently documents a shared Synology microscopy server [S13]; Kajima provides direct engineering NAS deployment evidence [S06]. Particular deployments do not establish sector penetration.

**Strongest counterevidence:** Incumbents already operate across institutional storage: RSpace supports SMB-linked files, versions and persistent resource links [S16]; Axle catalogs existing NAS/SAN media [S04]. EMu/Articheck already manage condition evidence [S27–S28], while Procore/Pype manage approvals, revisions, collection and closeout [S07–S09]. File-aware general AI removes the artificial requirement to upload the whole NAS repeatedly [S29–S31]. Citation/contradiction/uncertainty tables alone are not a defensible differentiator.

**Single largest unresolved risk:** The residual job may disappear when a real user operates their incumbent correctly and gives general AI the same scoped folder. Public pages do not establish that this joint residual exists. This is an evidence verdict, not proof that no such customer exists.

1. **No authoritative-local cohort:** No real recurrent review has source originals/source-of-record relationship on NAS/shared storage. Backups/stale mirrors/staged exports alone fail.
2. **Incumbent owns full job:** Correct EMu/Articheck or Procore/Pype already provides identity, revision, completeness and review records with no residual. Do not misconfigure it to create a gap.
3. **File-aware AI removes friction:** Same authorized root/instructions/output location handles preparation, changed sources and disposition with no material extra human burden. Upload-only comparisons cannot rescue this.
4. **UGREEN cannot preserve source/read coverage:** In-scope inputs, partial/unread/truncated states, references or save verification cannot be trusted. A silently omitted relevant source is a blocker, not missing support.
5. **Only OCR plus table comparison:** A one-off identifier extraction leaves no demonstrated NAS direction. Synthetic complexity is no remedy.
6. **Unsupported parsing is essential:** Native BIM, RAW, microscopy, video frames or forensic images required for the real decision cannot be read through authorized actions. Exports demonstrate a different/weaker job.
7. **Required privacy but unverified route:** Actual permitted processing cannot be satisfied by the verified model route. File location is insufficient.
8. **Authority confused:** Pending proposal/stale export/derivative/filename becomes approval. A hash is no cure.
9. **False certainty:** Unread appendix becomes no support, mismatch becomes damage/noncompliance, or saved note becomes acceptance. Any such behavior fails bounded review.
